# Virunnu: first day for a caterer

For caterers in the trial. About 20 minutes. Do it with a real order if you have one.
If anything is unclear, call: ______________ (Virunnu support).

## 1. Sign in
- Open **virunnu.in** and tap **Sign in**. Use the phone number and password we sent you (check your email).

## 2. Set up your menu and prices
- Open **Menu & Prices**. Check your items and rates. Add or change what you need.
- Open **Settings**, then **Profile**, for your business name, UPI id and GST details. These appear on your bills.

## 3. Make an order
1. Tap **+ New**, then **New order**.
2. Pick the customer (**Parties**) or add a new one with name and phone.
3. Choose the **function date**, **place** and the items, with quantities.
4. Add servings if the event has more than one meal.
5. Save. The order is in **Orders**.

## 4. Quotation first (optional)
- **+ New**, then **New quotation** gives the customer a price before they confirm. Convert it into an order when they agree.

## 5. Bills and money received
1. Open the order and create the **invoice**.
2. Record each payment: amount and how it was paid.
3. Send the customer the **receipt link**. It opens without signing in.
- **Day Book** shows money in and out for any day. **Orders** shows what is still due.

## 6. Purchases, staff and expenses
- **+ New**, then **Record purchase**: what you bought from which supplier, and what you paid.
- **Parties** also holds suppliers and staff.
- **Add expense**: gas, rent, transport.
- **Reports** shows the month: sales, purchases, expenses and profit.

## 7. Backup (do this every week, and after a busy day)
1. Open **Settings**, then **Backup**.
2. Tap **Download backup**. Keep the file safe (computer, email).
3. If something is lost: **Restore from a backup**, choose the file, check the summary, confirm.
- Only the main owner can download or restore. Team logins cannot.

## Rules to remember
- Only the main owner adds team logins (**Settings**, **Team logins**) and sees **Activity**.
- Virunnu does not take your customers' catering payments. You collect them yourself and record them here.
- Tell us about anything that looks wrong, with the date and the party's name.
