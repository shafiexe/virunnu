# Virunnu: first day for a convention centre

For hall owners in the trial. About 20 minutes. Do it with a real booking if you have one.
If anything is unclear, call: ______________ (Virunnu support).

## 1. Sign in
- Open **virunnu.in** and tap **Sign in**. Use the phone number and password we sent you (check your email).
- Change nothing else yet.

## 2. Check your hall
- Open **Settings**, then **Venue**. Check the hall name, price, photos and time slots (for example Morning, Evening).
- Wrong something? Change it there. Customers see this page.

## 3. Make a booking (someone phoned you)
1. Tap **+ New**, then **New booking** (or open **Calendar** and tap a date).
2. Choose the **date** and the **slot**.
3. Type the customer's **name** and **phone**, the **total**, and any **advance** received.
4. Save. The date is now blocked for other customers.

## 4. Record money received
1. Open **Bookings** and tap the booking.
2. Tap **Record payment**. Enter the amount and how it was paid (cash, UPI, bank).
3. Send the customer the **receipt link** from the booking page. It opens without signing in.
4. To remind a customer about a balance, tap **Send reminder**.
- A payment entered by mistake can be removed by the main owner only.

## 5. Requests from the website
- Customers can ask for a date on your public page. Those appear under **Enquiries**.
- Approve a request and it becomes a booking. Reject it and the date is free again.

## 6. Expenses and reports
- **+ New**, then **Add expense**: electricity, cleaning, salaries.
- **Reports** shows each month: what was billed, GST, money received, expenses and profit. You can download it as an Excel file for your accountant.

## 7. Online payments (optional)
- If a customer pays on the website, the money goes to Virunnu first. Virunnu keeps a **1% service fee**, and the payment company (Razorpay) takes its own fee from your share. The rest is sent to you.
- **Reports** shows "Paid online", "Virunnu's 1%", "Razorpay fee", "Sent to you" and "Still to come".
- You never have to use online payment. Cash and UPI to you directly work as before.

## 8. Backup (do this every week, and after a busy day)
1. Open **Settings**, then **Backup**.
2. Tap **Download backup**. Keep the file safe (computer, email).
3. If something is lost: **Restore from a backup**, choose the file, check the summary, confirm.
- Only the main owner can download or restore. Team logins cannot.

## Rules to remember
- Only the main owner adds team logins (**Settings**, **Team logins**) and sees **Activity**.
- Never share your password. Give staff their own login.
- Tell us about anything that looks wrong, with the date and the customer's name.
